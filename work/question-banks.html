Teaching

Practice Question Banks

Role
Author
Stack
Python, C and SQL content; Word and Markdown
AI used
Helps pick or write similar questions and builds the files; I decide and check every question
Outcome
Question banks, notes, practice sets and exam sets for my batches

Problem

One syllabus, many different gaps. A whole class can follow the same chapter while each student is stuck on something different. One fixed worksheet cannot serve all of them.

Constraints

Students come from different school boards (WBCHSE, CBSE and ICSE), and many read in both English and Bengali, so the material has to stay clear in both. Student names and details never appear in anything I publish.

How it’s made

I supply the context: book pages, website images and reference questions. AI helps pick questions from those, or write similar ones, and builds the question-bank files in Word and Markdown. I decide what goes in, and I check every question before a student sees it.

Result

I now write question banks, study notes, practice sets and exam sets for my batches. They range from whole-syllabus coverage to specific topics and individual students’ weak spots, in Python, C and SQL.

What I’d teach from it

Good practice moves in small steps. Start with the basics of a topic, then add one idea at a time, so a student builds confidence before the harder questions.
