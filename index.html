01 About

      
From tutor to co-founder.

      

        

          
I started as a freelance tutor, teaching mathematics, science and computer subjects to school students. Today I teach Computer Science and Application at Ganitasastram, the institute I co-founded and help run, including its website, events and digital content.

          
Teaching showed me what students actually struggle with. So I build things to fix it: a classroom management app for batches, attendance, tests and fees, practice material in Python, C and SQL, and lessons on my YouTube channel.

          
Next, I'm going deeper into data science and AI to become a stronger developer and a better teacher.
