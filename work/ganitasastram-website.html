Ganitasastram Website Redesign

Role
Co-founder, designer and developer
Stack
Wix, Velo (ganitasastram.com)
AI used
Defines a better structure and builds pages from the design system; I list needs and review
Outcome
Ongoing redesign of the live site, focused on admissions and student results

Problem

The institute’s website was bland. It did not guide a visitor towards enquiring, and it did not show what students achieve.

Constraints

The site runs on Wix with Velo, and it has to stay live while I edit it. Because it is the institute’s public face, changes are made carefully and reviewed before they go out.

What changed

I started by listing what the site needs:

  • Student admission forms
  • A student notes section
  • A hall of fame for student results
  • A consistent Ganitasastram design system across every page

How AI was used

I list the needs and supply all the context, including the Ganitasastram design system. AI proposes a better structure and builds the pages using that design system. I review the result before it is published.

Result

The redesign is ongoing. The site now has a clearer structure and a consistent design system, with a focus on admissions and student results.

What I’d teach from it

Why a design system matters: decide colours, type and spacing once, and every new page comes out consistent. It is the same idea as reusing a function instead of rewriting it.
